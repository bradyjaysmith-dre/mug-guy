The guy behind the mugs

Every mug has a story. I find them so you don't have to — combing thrift stores, estate sales, and classifieds across the Salt Lake Valley for pieces that are weird, wonderful, or just too good to leave on the shelf.

Mugs from diners that no longer exist. Novelty mugs from decades past. Studio ceramics made by hand. Souvenirs from places you'll never visit. Each one gets cleaned up and listed individually on eBay with honest photos and descriptions.

Based in SLC. Shipping from Utah. Usually pretty fast.
